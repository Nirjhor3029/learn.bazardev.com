# Chapter 14 — Reported (Indirect) Speech

> **Course:** IELTS Grammar · **Chapter:** 14 of 18
> Read on the website: [learn.bazardev.com/ielts-grammar/14-reported-speech](https://learn.bazardev.com/ielts-grammar/14-reported-speech)

Reported speech describes what someone said, without quoting their exact words. Three things usually shift together: the **tense** (one step back — called "backshift"), **pronouns**, and **time/place words**.

## 2. Backshift: the tense moves one step into the past

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| present simple ("I **work** here") | past simple ("he said he **worked** there") |
| present continuous ("I **am working**") | past continuous ("he said he **was working**") |
| present perfect ("I **have worked**") | past perfect ("he said he **had worked**") |
| past simple ("I **worked**") | past perfect ("he said he **had worked**") |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| must | had to *(or stays "must")* |

**Exception — no backshift needed when the fact is still true or general:** *"The sun rises in the east," she said* → *She said (that) the sun **rises** in the east.* (still a fact — backshifting is optional, though safer to backshift for exam writing).

## 3. Time and place words also shift

| Direct | Reported |
|---|---|
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before |
| tomorrow | the next/following day |
| this | that |
| here | there |
| ago | before |
| next week | the following week |

## 4. Pronouns and possessives shift to match the new speaker

*"**I** lost **my** keys,"* Rahim said → Rahim said **he** had lost **his** keys. The pronoun changes from the speaker's own point of view to the reporter's point of view.

## 5. Reporting questions and commands

- **Reported questions** keep statement word order (covered fully in Chapter 8): *"Where do you live?"* → *She asked where I lived.*
- **Reported commands/requests** use **tell/ask + object + infinitive**: *"Close the door,"* he said → He **told me to close** the door. *"Please help me,"* she said → She **asked me to help** her.
- **Negative commands**: *"Don't be late,"* he said → He told me **not to be** late.

## 6. Common mistakes to fix

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| She said she is tired now. | She said she **was** tired **then**. *(backshift the tense AND the time word)* |
| He told to me to wait. | He **told me** to wait. *("tell" takes a direct object, no "to" before it)* |
| She asked me where did I live. | She asked me where **I lived**. *(statement word order — see Chapter 8)* |
| He said that he will come. | He said that he **would** come. *("will" backshifts to "would")* |

## 7. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Report: "I am tired," she said.</strong></summary>

**She said (that) she was tired.** — "am" backshifts to "was", and the pronoun shifts to "she".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Report: "I will call you tomorrow," he said.</strong></summary>

**He said (that) he would call me the next day.** — "will" → "would", "tomorrow" → "the next day".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Fix: "He told to me to wait here."</strong></summary>

**He told me to wait there.** — "tell" takes a direct object with no "to"; "here" also shifts to "there" in reported speech.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. Report the command: "Don't touch that," she said.</strong></summary>

**She told me not to touch that/it.** — negative commands use "not to + base verb".
</details>
