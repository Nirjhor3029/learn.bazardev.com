# Chapter 8 — Task 2: Thesis Statement and Topic Sentences

> **Course:** IELTS Writing · **Chapter:** 8 of 12
> Read on the website: [learn.bazardev.com/ielts-writing/08-task2-thesis-and-topic-sentences](https://learn.bazardev.com/ielts-writing/08-task2-thesis-and-topic-sentences)

The thesis (outline) statement in your introduction makes a promise to the reader about what the essay will argue. Each body paragraph's topic sentence then has to **keep** that promise. When they don't match, the essay feels disorganised even if every sentence is grammatically correct.

A thesis statement must be **a clear statement, not a question**, and should be concise — one to two sentences is enough.

## 3. Thesis templates by question type (see Chapter 6)

| Type | Template |
|---|---|
| Opinion | *While some believe X, I would argue that Y, mainly because of [reason 1] and [reason 2].* |
| Discussion | *This essay will examine both perspectives before concluding that...* |
| Problem-solution | *This essay will explore the main causes of this problem and suggest several possible solutions.* |
| Advantage-disadvantage | *This essay will discuss both the benefits and drawbacks of X.* |
| Two-part | *This essay will address both why this occurs and what measures could reduce it.* |

## 4. Writing topic sentences that actually work

A good topic sentence names the idea **and** connects it to the main topic — not just a generic filler.

| ❌ Vague | ✅ Specific |
|---|---|
| There are many reasons for this. | Remote work improves employees' work-life balance by eliminating commute time. |
| This is an important issue. | One major drawback of remote work is the risk of social isolation. |

## 5. Common mistakes to fix

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| Is remote work good for everyone? *(thesis written as a question)* | **I believe remote work benefits most employees**, for the following reasons. *(a clear statement, not a question)* |
| The thesis promises "two reasons," but the essay only discusses one. | Make sure every point promised in the thesis gets its own body paragraph. |
| Topic sentence: "Remote work is popular nowadays." *(too generic)* | Topic sentence: "Remote work reduces commuting stress, which improves overall job satisfaction." |
| The thesis is 4 sentences long. | Keep the thesis to 1-2 sentences — save the detail for the body. |

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fix: "Is remote work good for everyone?" as a thesis statement.</strong></summary>

**I believe remote work benefits most employees, for the following reasons.** — a thesis must be a clear statement, not a question.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. A thesis promises two reasons, but the essay only develops one in the body. What's wrong?</strong></summary>

**The essay breaks its promise to the reader** — every point in the thesis needs its own body paragraph.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Which topic sentence is more specific: "This is an important issue" or "Remote work increases the risk of social isolation"?</strong></summary>

**"Remote work increases the risk of social isolation"** — it names a concrete idea, not a vague filler.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. How long should a thesis statement typically be?</strong></summary>

**1-2 sentences** — concise, not a paragraph of its own.
</details>
