The official IELTS band descriptors require "a relevant overview" to be **attempted** for Band 6, and "a clear overview" for Band 7+. Skipping it entirely — the single most common and most costly Task 1 mistake — holds your Task Achievement score down regardless of how accurate or detailed the rest of your report is.

To find your 2-3 features, ask: *What's the overall direction? What's the highest/lowest? Is there one standout change or exception?*

## 3. Overview sentence templates

| Starter | Example |
|---|---|
| Overall, it is clear that... | *Overall, it is clear that internet usage increased across all age groups.* |
| Overall, X and Y followed a similar/opposite trend, while Z... | *Overall, both cities saw rising populations, while the smallest town's population declined.* |
| In general,... | *In general, spending on education rose steadily, whereas spending on defence fell.* |
| The most noticeable feature is... | *The most noticeable feature is the sharp increase in online sales after 2015.* |

## 4. What stays OUT of the overview

- **No specific numbers** — "increased from 20% to 45%" belongs in the body. The overview can use comparative words (*highest, lowest, by far the largest*) but not exact figures.
- **No opinion** — "this is a positive trend" has no place in an objective report.
- **Not every category** — if there are six lines on a graph, group them (*most rose, except for X*), don't list all six.

## 5. Common mistakes to fix

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| *(no overview at all, straight into the data)* | Always include 1-2 overview sentences before the detailed body paragraphs. |
| Overall, sales increased from 200 to 450 units. | Overall, sales **increased significantly** over the period. *(save exact numbers for the body)* |
| Overall, this is a worrying trend. | Overall, **the data show a clear upward trend.** *(no opinion)* |
| The overview describes every single category in detail. | The overview stays short — 1-2 sentences, 2-3 features only. |

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. True or false: the overview should include exact percentages.</strong></summary>

**False** — exact figures belong in the body paragraphs; the overview uses comparative language instead.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Where does the overview go in the four-paragraph structure?</strong></summary>

**Right after the paraphrased introduction, before the body paragraphs.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Fix: "Overall, this is a worrying trend."</strong></summary>

**Overall, the data show a clear upward trend.** (or similar) — remove the opinion ("worrying").
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. What's the minimum band requirement that specifically mentions the overview?</strong></summary>

**Band 6** requires a relevant overview to be attempted; Band 7 requires it to be clear.
</details>
