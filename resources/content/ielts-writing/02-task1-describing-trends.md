Line and bar graphs that change over time are the most common Task 1 prompt. The skill isn't describing every data point — it's picking the right trend words and reporting only the features that matter.

**The overview is the single most important sentence for Task Achievement.** Examiners specifically look for it — write it right after your paraphrase, before any numbers, identifying the 2-3 biggest-picture features (the highest/lowest, the overall trend, the most dramatic change).

## 3. Trend vocabulary, by direction and strength

| Direction | Verbs | Nouns |
|---|---|---|
| Up (strong) | surge, rocket, soar | a surge, a sharp rise |
| Up (moderate) | rise, increase, grow, climb | a rise, an increase, growth |
| Down (strong) | plummet, plunge, collapse | a sharp fall, a plunge |
| Down (moderate) | fall, decrease, decline, drop | a fall, a decrease, a decline |
| No clear direction | fluctuate | fluctuation |
| Staying the same | remain stable, level off, plateau, stagnate | stability, a plateau |

**Degree adverbs** (pair with the verb): *dramatically, sharply, significantly* (big changes) · *steadily, gradually, moderately* (medium) · *slightly, marginally* (small).

## 4. Prepositions that carry exact data

- **increase by** + amount: *Sales increased **by** 20%.* (the size of the change)
- **increase to** + final number: *Sales increased **to** 500 units.* (the end point)
- **from...to**: *Sales rose **from** 300 **to** 500 units.* (start and end together)
- **between...and** / **from...to** (time): *between 2015 and 2020*, *from 2015 to 2020*

## 5. Common mistakes to fix

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| The graph show that sales increase. | The graph **shows** that sales **increased**. *(subject-verb agreement + correct tense — see IELTS Grammar Ch. 7 and Ch. 4/5)* |
| Sales increased dramatically slightly. | Sales **increased slightly**. *(don't stack two degree adverbs)* |
| I think sales will keep rising. | *(no opinion in Task 1 — describe only what the graph shows)* |
| The report describes every single number on the graph. | Group similar data and report only the **key features** — not every data point. |

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Which verb suits a dramatic, sudden increase: "rose" or "rocketed"?</strong></summary>

**Rocketed** — it signals a much sharper, more dramatic increase than "rose".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Fix: "Sales increased by 500 units." (if 500 is the final total, not the size of the change)</strong></summary>

**Sales increased to 500 units.** — "to" marks the end point; "by" marks the size of the change.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Where does the overview go: before or after the body paragraphs?</strong></summary>

**Before** — right after the paraphrased introduction, and before any detailed numbers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. Should the overview include specific numbers?</strong></summary>

**No** — the overview summarises the 2-3 biggest-picture features; specific numbers belong in the body paragraphs.
</details>
