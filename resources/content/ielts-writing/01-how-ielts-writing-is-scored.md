# Chapter 1 — How IELTS Writing Is Scored, and What Task 1 vs Task 2 Actually Want

> **Course:** IELTS Writing · **Chapter:** 1 of 12
> Read on the website: [learn.bazardev.com/ielts-writing/01-how-ielts-writing-is-scored](https://learn.bazardev.com/ielts-writing/01-how-ielts-writing-is-scored)

Before writing a single practice essay, it's worth knowing exactly what the examiner is scoring — because the two tasks are judged on the same four criteria but reward completely different things. This chapter is the foundation the rest of the course builds on, the same way Chapter 1 of IELTS Grammar and IELTS Vocabulary laid groundwork before the topic-by-topic chapters.

**The one thing to remember from this chart: Task 2 is worth twice as much as Task 1.** If you're short on time, protect Task 2's 40 minutes first.

## 3. The one golden rule for each task

- **Task 1's golden rule: describe, don't explain.** Report what the data shows — trends, comparisons, the most striking features — without guessing *why* those trends happened (unless the prompt explicitly asks) and without giving your opinion.
- **Task 2's golden rule: answer every part of the question.** A question with two parts ("discuss both views **and** give your opinion") loses marks on Task Response if you only do one part, no matter how well-written the rest of the essay is.

## 4. A simple time budget

| | Task 1 | Task 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Plan | ~3 minutes: identify key features / decide your position | ~5 minutes: identify the question type, plan 2-3 main points |
| Write | ~13 minutes | ~30 minutes |
| Check | ~4 minutes: grammar, spelling, word count | ~5 minutes: did you answer every part? |

## 5. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Which two of the four criteria does IELTS Grammar most directly help with?</strong></summary>

**Grammatical Range and Accuracy** (directly) — and indirectly Coherence and Cohesion, since Chapter 16 of IELTS Grammar covers linking words.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. If you only have 10 minutes left and haven't started Task 2, what should you prioritise?</strong></summary>

**Task 2** — it's worth 66% of the Writing score, double Task 1's 33%.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. True or false: Task 1 should include your personal opinion on the data.</strong></summary>

**False** — Task 1 asks for an objective description, not an opinion.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. A Task 2 question asks you to discuss both views and give your opinion. You only discuss both views. What happens to your score?</strong></summary>

**It's capped lower on Task Response** — missing part of the question is penalised regardless of writing quality elsewhere.
</details>
