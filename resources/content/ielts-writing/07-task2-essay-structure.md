**Worked mini-example** (topic: technology in education):

*"Topic sentence:* Online learning platforms have made education far more accessible. *Explanation:* Students no longer need to live near a university or pay for accommodation to access high-quality teaching. *Example:* For instance, platforms like Coursera let students in rural areas study courses from top universities for free."

## 3. What each part should (and shouldn't) do

- **Introduction:** paraphrase the question in your own words (don't copy it), then add an **outline statement** previewing your two main points or your opinion (detailed in Chapter 8).
- **Body paragraphs:** one main idea each — don't cram two unrelated ideas into one paragraph, and don't split one idea across two paragraphs.
- **Conclusion:** restate your position/main points in different words — **no new arguments or evidence** belong here, and it shouldn't just repeat the introduction word-for-word.

## 4. Common mistakes to fix

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| The whole essay is one long paragraph with no breaks. | Use clear paragraph breaks — four paragraphs, one idea per body paragraph. |
| The introduction copies the question's exact wording. | Paraphrase the question using your own vocabulary and structure. |
| The conclusion introduces a brand-new argument. | The conclusion only restates what's already been argued — nothing new. |
| The essay is only 180 words. | Aim for 250+ words using the paragraph-length guide above as a target, not a ceiling. |

## 5. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Which part of the essay should introduce a brand-new argument?</strong></summary>

**None of them — new arguments belong in body paragraphs, never the conclusion.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. What are the three parts of a body paragraph?</strong></summary>

**Topic sentence, explanation, example.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Roughly how many words should a 250-300 word essay's introduction be?</strong></summary>

**About 40-60 words** — short, just the paraphrase plus an outline statement.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. True or false: the introduction should copy the question's exact wording.</strong></summary>

**False** — paraphrase it in your own words instead.
</details>
