Process diagrams and maps are the only Task 1 chart types with **no numbers at all**. Processes test your passive voice and sequencing; maps test spatial and change vocabulary.

**Why passive voice?** Process steps rarely name *who* performs the action, and the focus is on the step itself — exactly the use case covered in IELTS Grammar Chapter 11. *"The raw material is collected"* (not "Someone collects the raw material").

**Sequencing words**, roughly in order of how far through the process you are:

| Position | Words |
|---|---|
| Start | first, firstly, initially, to begin with |
| Middle | next, then, after this/that, following this, subsequently, once X has happened |
| End | finally, lastly, at the final stage |
| Cyclic process | the cycle then repeats, the process begins again |

**Spatial/location language:** *to the north/south/east/west of, adjacent to, in the centre of, on the outskirts of, along the coast, opposite, next to.*

**Change verbs** (almost always passive, like processes): *was built/constructed, was demolished, was replaced by, was converted into, was extended, was relocated, remained unchanged, disappeared.*

*"The old factory **was demolished** and **was replaced by** a shopping centre, which **is located** to the north of the river."*

## 3. The same four-paragraph structure applies

- **Paragraph 1:** paraphrase the question (number of steps / the two time periods shown).
- **Paragraph 2 (overview):** for a process — how many steps, start and end point; for a map — the single biggest change.
- **Paragraphs 3-4:** the detailed steps or changes, in order.

## 4. Common mistakes to fix

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| Someone collect the raw material first. | **First, the raw material is collected.** *(passive voice, correct tense/agreement)* |
| The park change to a car park. | The park **was converted into** a car park. *(correct change verb + passive)* |
| The factory is north the river. | The factory is **to the north of** the river. *(preposition needs "to...of")* |
| I think this process is efficient. | *(no opinion — Task 1 still reports data only)* |

## 5. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fix: "Someone collect the raw material first."</strong></summary>

**First, the raw material is collected.** — passive voice, no named doer, correct tense.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Which verb describes a building being torn down: "demolished" or "extended"?</strong></summary>

**Demolished.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Fix: "The factory is north the river."</strong></summary>

**The factory is to the north of the river.** — the full preposition phrase is needed.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. Which sequencing word fits the very end of a process?</strong></summary>

**Finally** (or "lastly").
</details>
