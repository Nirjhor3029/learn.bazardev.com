# Chapter 4 — Technology and the Internet Vocabulary

> **Course:** IELTS Vocabulary · **Chapter:** 4 of 12
> Read on the website: [learn.bazardev.com/ielts-vocabulary/04-technology-and-the-internet-vocabulary](https://learn.bazardev.com/ielts-vocabulary/04-technology-and-the-internet-vocabulary)

Technology is a near-guaranteed IELTS topic — Writing Task 2 loves "to what extent does technology bring more harm than good" style questions, and Speaking Part 3 regularly asks about social media, AI, or online learning. This chapter is built around that double-edged framing: most tech vocabulary splits into **benefits** and **risks**.

## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by theme

**Access and infrastructure**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **cutting-edge technology** | *The hospital invested in cutting-edge technology to improve diagnosis.* |
| **high-speed internet** | *Access to high-speed internet remains limited in rural areas.* |
| **bridge the digital divide** | *Government programmes aim to bridge the digital divide between urban and rural communities.* |
| **artificial intelligence (AI)** | *Advances in artificial intelligence are transforming entire industries.* |

**Risks and concerns**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **overreliance on technology** | *Critics warn of an overreliance on technology among young people.* |
| **a data privacy breach** | *The company faced criticism after a major data privacy breach.* |
| **a rise in cybercrime** | *Experts have reported a sharp rise in cybercrime in recent years.* |
| **excessive screen time** | *Excessive screen time has been linked to poor sleep quality in children.* |
| **job displacement** | *Automation has caused job displacement in manufacturing.* |

**Benefits and solutions**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **streamline processes** | *New software has helped the company streamline its processes.* |
| **boost productivity** | *Remote collaboration tools have helped boost productivity.* |
| **remote work** | *Remote work has become increasingly common since the pandemic.* |
| **online learning platforms** | *Online learning platforms give students access to courses from top universities.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| online vs. offline | connected to the internet vs. not connected |
| face-to-face vs. virtual communication | in-person interaction vs. interaction through a screen |
| manual labour vs. automation | work done by people vs. work done by machines |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use)

*"While artificial intelligence has helped many companies streamline processes and boost productivity, critics argue that overreliance on technology and the risk of job displacement mean its benefits are not shared equally."*

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fill in: "Government programmes aim to ___ the digital divide." (bridge / build)</strong></summary>

**bridge** — "bridge the digital divide" is the fixed collocation.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Which word family member fits: "The factory ___ its entire assembly line last year." (automate / automation / automated)</strong></summary>

**automated** — the past simple of the verb "automate".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. "A sharp rise in ___" refers to online crimes like hacking and fraud. (cybercrime / cyberspace)</strong></summary>

**cybercrime** — "a rise in cybercrime" is the standard collocation.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. Which pair contrasts in-person interaction with interaction through a screen?</strong></summary>

**Face-to-face vs. virtual communication.**
</details>
