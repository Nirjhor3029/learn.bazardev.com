Education comes up constantly across all four IELTS skills — Speaking Part 1 almost always asks about your studies, Writing Task 2 regularly sets essay questions about schooling, and Listening/Reading passages often describe universities or training schemes. This chapter applies the six methods from Chapter 1: word families, example sentences, collocations, all grouped under one topic.

## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by theme

**Access and structure of education**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **compulsory education** | *Compulsory education in Bangladesh runs from age 6 to 10.* |
| **higher education** | *Enrolment in higher education has doubled over the past decade.* |
| **vocational training** | *Vocational training equips students with a practical, job-ready skill.* |
| **rising tuition fees** | *Rising tuition fees have made university less accessible for many.* |
| **a broad curriculum** | *The new curriculum includes financial literacy alongside core subjects.* |

**Outcomes and quality**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **literacy rate** | *The country's literacy rate has improved significantly since 2000.* |
| **dropout rate** | *Free school meals have been shown to reduce the dropout rate.* |
| **academic performance** | *Lack of sleep is linked to poorer academic performance.* |
| **extracurricular activities** | *Extracurricular activities help students develop teamwork skills.* |
| **lifelong learning** | *Employers increasingly value a commitment to lifelong learning.* |
| **accused of plagiarism** | *The student was accused of plagiarism after copying an online essay.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| state school vs. private school | state school is government-funded and free; private school charges fees |
| literate vs. illiterate | able to read and write vs. unable to |
| disadvantaged/underprivileged students | students with fewer resources or opportunities than their peers |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use, method 6)

*"Although compulsory education guarantees every child a basic level of schooling, disadvantaged students often still fall behind in academic performance, partly because they can't afford the extracurricular activities that wealthier peers take for granted."*

Notice how many collocations from this chapter fit naturally into a single, exam-ready sentence — that's exactly the goal.

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fill in: "The government wants to improve the ___ rate in rural areas." (literacy / literate)</strong></summary>

**literacy** — "literacy rate" is the fixed collocation; "literate" is an adjective describing a person.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Which word family member fits: "She finally ___ as a doctor last year." (qualify / qualification / qualified)</strong></summary>

**qualified** — this is the past simple of the verb "qualify" ("she qualified"), not the adjective or the noun.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. "___ training" prepares someone for a specific trade, not an academic degree. (Vocational / Higher)</strong></summary>

**Vocational** — "vocational training" = practical, job-specific training.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. Which is the opposite of "state school"?</strong></summary>

**Private school** — government-funded and free vs. fee-charging.
</details>
