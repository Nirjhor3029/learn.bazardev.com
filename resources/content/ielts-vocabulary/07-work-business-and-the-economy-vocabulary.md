Work and the economy appear constantly in Speaking Part 1 (your job, your studies) and in Writing Task 2 essays about unemployment, wages, and economic growth. This chapter groups vocabulary around the **business cycle** — the natural rise and fall of an economy over time.

## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by theme

**Employment**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **job satisfaction** | *Job satisfaction tends to be higher when employees feel valued.* |
| **the unemployment rate** | *The unemployment rate fell to its lowest level in a decade.* |
| **a competitive salary** | *The company offers a competitive salary to attract top talent.* |
| **job security** | *Many young workers worry about a lack of job security.* |
| **made redundant** | *Hundreds of workers were made redundant after the factory closed.* |

**Business and the economy**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **economic growth** | *Sustained economic growth has lifted millions out of poverty.* |
| **a thriving economy** | *Singapore is often cited as an example of a thriving economy.* |
| **foreign investment** | *Tax incentives have helped attract foreign investment.* |
| **enter a recession** | *Several major economies entered a recession last year.* |
| **income inequality** | *Widening income inequality remains a major political issue.* |

**Working conditions**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **long working hours** | *Long working hours have been linked to burnout and poor health.* |
| **flexible working arrangements** | *Flexible working arrangements have become far more common since 2020.* |
| **raise the minimum wage** | *Several states have voted to raise the minimum wage.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| boom vs. recession | strong growth, low unemployment vs. economic decline, rising unemployment |
| developed vs. developing economy | high industrialisation and income vs. still industrialising, lower income |
| blue-collar vs. white-collar jobs | manual/industrial labour vs. office/professional work |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use)

*"Although foreign investment has driven strong economic growth in recent years, critics point out that widening income inequality means many workers still face job insecurity and long working hours without a corresponding rise in job satisfaction."*

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fill in: "The factory increased its ___ of cars by 20% this year." (produce / production / productive)</strong></summary>

**production** — the noun form fits after "its".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. "Hundreds of workers were made ___ after the factory closed." (redundant / recession)</strong></summary>

**redundant** — meaning they lost their jobs because they were no longer needed.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Which stage of the business cycle follows a recession?</strong></summary>

**Recovery** — the economy begins growing again after a recession.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. Which pair contrasts manual/industrial labour with office/professional work?</strong></summary>

**Blue-collar vs. white-collar jobs.**
</details>
