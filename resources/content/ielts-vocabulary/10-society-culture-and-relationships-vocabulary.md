## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by theme

**Family and community**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **a close-knit community** | *Villages are often described as close-knit communities.* |
| **the extended family** | *The extended family plays a far bigger role in rural areas.* |
| **a nuclear family** | *Urbanisation has led to a shift from extended to nuclear families.* |
| **the generation gap** | *A growing generation gap has emerged over attitudes to technology.* |
| **social cohesion** | *Shared community events help strengthen social cohesion.* |

**Diversity and relationships**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **cultural diversity** | *The city is known for celebrating its cultural diversity.* |
| **gender equality** | *Many countries have made progress toward gender equality.* |
| **social discrimination** | *Minority groups still face social discrimination in some regions.* |
| **peer pressure** | *Teenagers often struggle with peer pressure.* |

**Social change**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **rapid urbanisation** | *Rapid urbanisation has transformed traditional community structures.* |
| **social mobility** | *Access to education can significantly improve social mobility.* |
| **social isolation** | *Elderly people living alone often experience social isolation.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| nuclear family vs. extended family | parents and children only vs. multiple generations/relatives living together |
| individualism vs. collectivism | valuing personal achievement vs. valuing group harmony and obligation |
| urban vs. rural community | densely populated, less close-knit vs. smaller, more close-knit |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use)

*"Rapid urbanisation has led many societies to shift from the extended family to the nuclear family, and some sociologists argue this shift has weakened social cohesion and contributed to social isolation, particularly among the elderly."*

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fill in: "The company was accused of ___ against older applicants." (discriminate / discrimination / discriminatory)</strong></summary>

**discrimination** — the noun form fits after "accused of".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Which term describes a family of parents and children only, without other relatives?</strong></summary>

**A nuclear family.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Which culture type values group harmony and family obligation over personal achievement?</strong></summary>

**Collectivist culture.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. "Elderly people living alone often experience social ___." (isolation / mobility)</strong></summary>

**isolation** — "social isolation" is the fixed collocation for lacking social contact.
</details>
