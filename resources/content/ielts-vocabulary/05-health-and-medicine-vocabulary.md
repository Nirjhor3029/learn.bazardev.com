## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by theme

**Illness and risk factors**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **chronic illness** | *Many people now live with a chronic illness for decades thanks to modern medicine.* |
| **underlying health condition** | *People with an underlying health condition are more vulnerable to complications.* |
| **a sedentary lifestyle** | *A sedentary lifestyle significantly increases the risk of heart disease.* |
| **exhibit symptoms** | *Patients who exhibit symptoms are advised to seek medical attention immediately.* |

**Healthcare and prevention**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **access to healthcare** | *Equal access to healthcare remains a major challenge in rural areas.* |
| **life expectancy** | *Improved sanitation has significantly increased life expectancy worldwide.* |
| **preventive measures** | *Washing hands regularly is one of the simplest preventive measures.* |
| **a balanced diet** | *A balanced diet provides the body with the nutrients it needs.* |
| **public health campaign** | *The government launched a public health campaign to reduce smoking.* |

**Mental health**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **mental health issues** | *Schools are increasingly focused on raising awareness of mental health issues.* |
| **stress-related illness** | *Stress-related illnesses have risen alongside longer working hours.* |
| **work-life balance** | *A healthy work-life balance helps prevent burnout.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| physical health vs. mental health | the body's condition vs. emotional/psychological wellbeing |
| acute vs. chronic illness | sudden and short-term vs. long-lasting or recurring |
| preventive vs. curative care | stopping illness before it happens vs. treating it after |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use)

*"Although improved access to healthcare has increased life expectancy worldwide, many experts argue that preventive measures, such as a balanced diet and regular exercise, are more cost-effective than curative treatment for a sedentary, modern lifestyle."*

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fill in: "Doctors made an early ___ after running several tests." (diagnose / diagnosis / diagnostic)</strong></summary>

**diagnosis** — the noun form is needed after "an early".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. "A ___ lifestyle significantly increases the risk of heart disease." (sedentary / sudden)</strong></summary>

**sedentary** — meaning involving little physical activity.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Which pair contrasts stopping illness before it happens with treating it afterward?</strong></summary>

**Preventive vs. curative care.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. "Washing hands regularly is one of the simplest ___ measures." (preventive / curative)</strong></summary>

**preventive** — "preventive measures" is the fixed collocation.
</details>
