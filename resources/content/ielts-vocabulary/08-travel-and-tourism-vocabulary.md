# Chapter 8 — Travel and Tourism Vocabulary

Travel is one of the most common Speaking Part 1 and Part 2 topics — describing a trip, a place you'd like to visit, your travel habits — and tourism's impact on local communities regularly appears in Writing Task 2. This chapter covers both the personal-experience vocabulary and the more analytical tourism-industry vocabulary.

## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by theme

**Personal travel experience**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **a breathtaking view** | *The hike rewarded us with a breathtaking view of the valley.* |
| **off the beaten track** | *She prefers destinations off the beaten track to crowded cities.* |
| **cultural immersion** | *Living with a local family offered real cultural immersion.* |
| **culture shock** | *Many travellers experience culture shock during their first few days abroad.* |

**Tourism industry**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **a tourist attraction** | *The temple has become a major tourist attraction.* |
| **boost the local economy** | *Tourism helps boost the local economy, especially in coastal towns.* |
| **tourist revenue** | *The island relies heavily on tourist revenue.* |
| **a package holiday** | *Many families prefer a package holiday for its convenience.* |
| **sustainable tourism** | *Sustainable tourism aims to minimise environmental impact.* |

**Negative impacts**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **overtourism** | *Overtourism has damaged several of the world's most popular sites.* |
| **cultural heritage** | *Local authorities are working to preserve the region's cultural heritage.* |
| **environmental degradation** | *Unchecked tourism can lead to environmental degradation.* |
| **commercialisation** | *Critics point to the commercialisation of traditional festivals for tourists.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| mass tourism vs. eco-tourism | large-scale, high-volume tourism vs. small-scale, environmentally conscious tourism |
| domestic vs. international tourism | travelling within one's own country vs. travelling abroad |
| authentic vs. commercialised experience | reflecting genuine local culture vs. adapted/staged for tourists |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use)

*"While tourism can boost the local economy and encourage cultural exchange, overtourism and the commercialisation of cultural heritage have led many destinations to promote sustainable, eco-friendly alternatives instead."*

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fill in: "Local authorities are working to ___ the region's cultural heritage." (preserve / preservation / preserved)</strong></summary>

**preserve** — the verb form is needed after "to".
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. "Many travellers experience ___ during their first few days abroad." (culture shock / cultural immersion)</strong></summary>

**culture shock** — the disorientation of encountering an unfamiliar culture.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Which pair contrasts large-scale tourism with small-scale, environmentally conscious tourism?</strong></summary>

**Mass tourism vs. eco-tourism.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. "She prefers destinations ___ to crowded cities." (off the beaten track / on the beaten path)</strong></summary>

**off the beaten track** — meaning unusual or less-visited places.
</details>
