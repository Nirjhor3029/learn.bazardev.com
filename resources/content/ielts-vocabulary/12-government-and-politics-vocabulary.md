## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by theme

**Government and governance**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **a democratically elected government** | *The country held its first democratically elected government in decades.* |
| **political stability** | *Foreign investors value political stability above almost everything else.* |
| **pass legislation** | *Parliament passed new legislation to tighten environmental standards.* |
| **government policy** | *Government policy on healthcare has shifted significantly in recent years.* |
| **cuts to public spending** | *Cuts to public spending have affected schools and hospitals alike.* |

**Political participation**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **the right to vote** | *Every citizen over 18 has the right to vote.* |
| **voter turnout** | *Voter turnout was notably low in the last election.* |
| **political apathy** | *Political apathy among young people remains a concern for many democracies.* |
| **hold a referendum** | *The government held a referendum on the proposed constitutional change.* |

**Issues and ideology**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **allegations of corruption** | *The minister resigned following allegations of corruption.* |
| **civil liberties** | *Civil liberties are often curtailed during states of emergency.* |
| **foreign policy** | *The country's foreign policy has grown more assertive in recent years.* |
| **political polarisation** | *Growing political polarisation has made compromise increasingly difficult.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| democracy vs. authoritarian regime | elected leadership, civil liberties vs. centralised, restricted power |
| centralised vs. decentralised government | decisions made nationally vs. distributed to local/regional bodies |
| left-wing vs. right-wing politics | typically favours more state intervention vs. typically favours less state intervention |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use)

*"While a democratically elected government is generally expected to protect civil liberties and remain accountable through free elections, declining voter turnout and rising political apathy have led some to question whether democratic institutions are functioning as intended."*

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. Fill in: "Parliament ___ new legislation to tighten environmental standards." (pass / passed / passing)</strong></summary>

**passed** — the past simple form fits this completed past action.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. "The minister resigned following allegations of ___." (corruption / legislation)</strong></summary>

**corruption** — "allegations of corruption" is the standard collocation.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Which pair contrasts elected leadership with centralised, restricted power?</strong></summary>

**Democracy vs. authoritarian regime.**
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. "Voter ___ was notably low in the last election." (turnout / apathy)</strong></summary>

**turnout** — "voter turnout" refers to the proportion of eligible voters who actually voted.
</details>
