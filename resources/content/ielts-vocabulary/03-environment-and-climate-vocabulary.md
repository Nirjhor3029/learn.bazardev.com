## 3. High-value collocations, grouped by stage

**Causes and human impact**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **carbon emissions** | *Many governments have pledged to cut carbon emissions by 2030.* |
| **burning fossil fuels** | *Burning fossil fuels is the single biggest driver of global warming.* |
| **widespread deforestation** | *Widespread deforestation in the Amazon has accelerated in recent years.* |
| **greenhouse gas emissions** | *Greenhouse gas emissions trap heat in the atmosphere.* |
| **non-renewable resources** | *Oil and coal are non-renewable resources that will eventually run out.* |

**Effects**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **the effects of global warming** | *The effects of global warming are already visible in melting glaciers.* |
| **rising sea levels** | *Rising sea levels threaten low-lying coastal cities.* |
| **endangered species** | *Habitat loss has pushed several endangered species toward extinction.* |
| **natural habitat** | *Urban expansion continues to destroy natural habitats.* |
| **extreme weather events** | *Extreme weather events are becoming more frequent and more severe.* |

**Solutions**

| Collocation | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **sustainable development** | *Sustainable development balances economic growth with environmental protection.* |
| **renewable energy** | *Investment in renewable energy has grown rapidly over the past decade.* |
| **reduce your carbon footprint** | *Simple changes, like cycling instead of driving, reduce your carbon footprint.* |
| **wildlife conservation efforts** | *Wildlife conservation efforts have helped several species recover.* |
| **encourage recycling** | *Local councils are running campaigns to encourage recycling.* |

## 4. Contrast pairs worth knowing

| Pair | Difference |
|---|---|
| renewable vs. non-renewable energy | can be replenished naturally (solar, wind) vs. finite and depletable (coal, oil) |
| climate change vs. climate crisis | "climate change" is the neutral/technical term; "climate crisis" signals urgency |
| man-made vs. natural disaster | caused by human activity vs. occurring without human cause |

## 5. Seeing it all combined (active use)

*"Although burning fossil fuels remains the main driver of greenhouse gas emissions, many governments are now investing in renewable energy and promoting sustainable development to reduce the long-term effects of global warming."*

## 6. Quick practice

<details>
<summary><strong>Q1. "Oil and coal are ___ resources." (renewable / non-renewable)</strong></summary>

**non-renewable** — they are finite and cannot be replenished naturally.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q2. Which word family member fits: "The river has become dangerously ___." (pollute / pollution / polluted)</strong></summary>

**polluted** — the adjective, describing the state of the river.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q3. Fill in: "Wildlife ___ efforts have helped several species recover." (conservation / conserve)</strong></summary>

**conservation** — "wildlife conservation efforts" is the fixed collocation (noun form).
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q4. Put in the right order: "greenhouse gas emissions", "burning fossil fuels", "global warming" — which causes which?</strong></summary>

**Burning fossil fuels → greenhouse gas emissions → global warming** — this is the standard cause-and-effect chain.
</details>
